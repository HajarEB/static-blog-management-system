TITLE Disobedient Children
DATE 06/12/2023
TAG  #Obedience

“Worship Allah and join none with Him in worship, and do good to parents, kinsfolk, orphans, Al-Masakin (the poor), the neighbour who is near of kin, the neighbour who is a stranger, the companion by your side, the wayfarer (you meet), and those (slaves) whom your right hands possess. Verily, Allah does not like such as are proud and boastful.”

[Quran; Surah an-Nisa’ 4:36]

In the above Ayah, can you see that Allah has mentioned parents right after He mentions worship to Him? And He mentions doing good to parents before mentioning being good to others? Note that this Ayah also mentions that Allah dislikes those who are proud and boastful. How often is someone who is arguing with his parents, proud and boastful?

In a Hadeeth, the Prophet (pbuh) was asked: “Which deed is the dearest to Allah?” He replied: “To perform the (daily compulsory) prayers at their (early) stated fixed time.” Then he was asked: “What is the next (in goodness)?” He replied: “To be good and dutiful to your parents.” (Al-Bukhari 527).

In another Hadeeth, the Prophet (pbuh) said,

“I guarantee a house in Jannah for one who gives up arguing, even if he is in the right; and I guarantee a home in the middle of Jannah for one who abandons lying even for the sake of fun; and I guarantee a house in the highest part of Jannah for one who has good manners.”

[Sunan Abu Dawud]

Briefly, this beautiful, authentic Hadeeth tells us that we should try to practise patience whenever we are about to enter an argument with someone. It also tells us that giving up lying is worthy of being rewarded with a mansion in the middle of Jannah! And lastly, but certainly not least, if we were to have good manners, we can get the highest part of Jannah, in shaa Allah!

Isn’t that wonderful?

But, let’s get real. How many of us are given obedient, loving children who do not talk back? Few, very few indeed. Especially for Muslims living in the western world. SubhaanAllah.

Rude children, or worse, rude children who lie, break their parents’ hearts. In Islam, it is not allowed to be disobedient to our parents. Nor is it allowed to lie.

Do our children know that? Are we one of those children?

Are we one of those parents who are encountering such problems? Or do you know of someone who is?
